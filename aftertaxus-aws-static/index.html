Before you start

Good questions. Straight answers.

Is the full AfterTaxUS app really free?

Yes. Both the investment comparison calculator and the full portfolio app are free to use. You can compare two investments without signing up. A free account is required to save and return to a portfolio workbook.

What does an after-tax investment calculator compare?

It estimates the income left after taxes on an investment’s income. Both options use the same investment amount. Change the yield, tax treatment, filing status and state to explore the difference. It does not predict price appreciation or compare investment risk.

Can a lower-yield investment leave me with more money?

Yes, depending on tax treatment and your circumstances. In the illustration above, $5,000 taxed at an assumed 30% leaves $3,500, while $4,000 assumed fully tax-exempt leaves $4,000. Actual exemptions, tax rates, fees and risk must be checked separately.

Which tax year and locations does it cover?

The current model is for 2025. The comparison includes federal and state estimates, with state selections across the U.S. Coverage and precision vary by rule and state. The full app adds supported local tax and W-2 payroll settings; neither tool models every tax, credit or special situation. Read the limitations.

Are the yields current, and is the “winner” a recommendation?

No. Sample yields are illustrative and should be replaced or verified with the issuer’s current information. The winner is the larger estimated after-tax income under your assumptions—not an assessment of risk, liquidity, fees, total return or suitability.

Do I need to connect my bank or an AI assistant?

No. You can enter your own numbers manually. AI/MCP access is optional and separate from using the website. Review the permissions before granting access to a private workbook. See our privacy policy.
