## Required NIIT and chart qualifications

The NIIT input contract is documented in `C:/myapp/docs/tax-niit-contract.md:1`:

- Explicit `capitalGains` without a NIIT ledger/aggregate derives capital-only NII and emits a scope warning. It does not invent investment income from wages, Social Security, exempt interest, or legacy preferred-income inputs. Explicit aggregate zero stays authoritative.
- Legacy `netInvestmentIncome` remains **pre-capital-loss**: modeled offsets/current deductible loss are applied once, and allowed expenses are already the caller's responsibility. Opt-in `netInvestmentIncomeMode: "final"` preserves a completed aggregate unchanged before MAGI-limited NIIT.
- An explicit investment ledger includes net capital once, allowed additional NIIT expenses once, and an optional signed NIIT-only capital adjustment. `niitDetails.source` identifies which reconciliation contract applies. An aggregate mode cannot coexist with a ledger; absent an explicit mode, legacy ledger precedence is diagnosed.
- The two HTTP fixtures establish $50,000 capital-only NII → $1,900 NIIT and final NII $23,000 → $874 under their synthetic high-MAGI assumptions, plus HTTP 400 for the conflicting representation. The capital-only case also passed against the deployed public endpoint without touching portfolio storage.
- **Remaining limitation:** dedicated UI controls and the MCP tax-input schema do not expose the advanced aggregate mode/full NIIT workflow. Adapters still need explicit schema, persistence, diagnostics, and regular-income reconciliation. Expense eligibility/allocation, participation/passive-loss rules, basis, and NIIT-specific exclusions/carryforwards are not automatically established by this engine.
